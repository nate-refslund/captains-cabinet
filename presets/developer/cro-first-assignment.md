# CRO First Assignment: Market Landscape & Competitive Intelligence

**Type:** Research assessment
**Priority:** P0 — complete before entering the recurring research-sweep cadence
**Deliverables:** Tier 2 notes, initial research brief, Warroom briefing

## Objective

Build a foundational understanding of the market, competition, and users; produce an initial intelligence package the CPO and CoS can use immediately.

## Instructions

1. **Absorb context.** Read the business-context docs the product config points to (`instance/config/product.yml`) — vision, strategy, positioning, growth guardrails — plus the CPO's roadmap and `shared/backlog.md`. Synthesis to `instance/memory/tier2/cro/market-context.md`.
2. **Map the competitive landscape.** Using the deployment's research/web-search tools, profile the product category's direct competitors, adjacent players, and emerging threats. Per competitor: name, positioning, key features, pricing, user base size (if available), strengths, weaknesses.
3. **Size the market.** Market size, growth trends, user behavior patterns, and technology trends relevant to the product's category.
4. **Write the initial research brief:** competitive positioning map, key differentiation, market gaps, risks, recommended priorities for future sweeps. Write to `shared/interfaces/research-briefs/` and publish to the knowledge base.
5. **Coordinate.** Post a Warroom summary; notify CPO and CoS via Redis (`notify-officer.sh`) that the initial intelligence package is ready.

## Success Criteria

- [ ] Synthesis in Tier 2; 5+ competitors profiled; market sizing captured
- [ ] Research brief published; Warroom briefed; CPO and CoS notified
