# My slice — ledger-api

I contribute to ledger-api. I do not own it: the owning team reviews and
releases my changes, and I have no say in its roadmap.

This folder is what I actually keep on disk.

- `repo/` — a checkout of the service. One of about forty in the estate.
- `tracker/` — a weekly CSV export of the rows assigned to me.
- `docs/` — the parts of the shared documentation space I need on call.

None of these is the whole picture. The repo is one service, the export is
only my rows, and the docs are a partial sync of a much larger wiki that
nobody reads end to end.
